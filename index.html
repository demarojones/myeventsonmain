Our Story

Where Vision
Meets Venue

Located in the heart of downtown Canton, Events on Main is a premier industrial event venue crafted for those who refuse the ordinary. Our Suite 100 space delivers raw, architectural character—exposed brick, soaring ceilings, and flexible layouts that adapt to any occasion.

Whether you're planning an intimate rehearsal dinner, a lavish wedding reception, a milestone birthday, or a corporate gathering, we provide the ideal backdrop alongside everything you need—including tables and chairs—to make the moment seamless.

The rental fee includes all day use of the boardwalk, main event space, 5 wine barrels, entrance table, cake table, 3 food bars, 2 three-layer carts, shared use of 2 bathrooms, 15 tables, and 124 chairs.

9am–11pm is the standard rental window (14 hours). An additional fee applies to extend to 1am. These end times mean no one remains in the building — all guests and vendors must be out by the end time.

No, a rehearsal is not included in the rental. Combo days are available, or you're welcome to come walk through and make plans on any day that doesn't have an event on the calendar.

We suggest 124 guests — that's why we supply that many chairs. The space is fire-marshalled for 175 if tables are not used.

Yes — 124 white folding chairs and 15 round 5' tables are included. Additional rectangular tables are available in the storage room. The 4' tables are height-adjustable (kid size or counter height), and a couple of 6' tables are also available.

We don't require specific vendors — you're free to bring in whomever you'd like.

Check the Events Calendar on this website. If you see a name on a date, it's been taken. We offer a one-week complimentary hold so you have time to view the space and collaborate on dates.

The salon downstairs will let you in 9am–7pm on any day there is not an event on the calendar. No appointment needed.

Text Majesty your email to receive a printable application and indemnity form. The salon's front desk accepts cash or check for ½ the rental fee — they will call Majesty to finalize and place your name on the calendar. It is your responsibility to confirm your name appears on the correct date. Checks should be made out to Events on Main. Zelle payments can also be arranged. We do not accept credit cards.

No refunds are available. However, we will mark your original date as "Movable" and open it to others. If you later choose a new date, your payment can be applied toward that future reservation.

The second half of your rental fee — plus any additional fees — is due 30 days before your event, along with a $500 refundable damage deposit.

Yes, alcohol is permitted. However, you are required to contact the Canton Police at 770-720-4883 to hire a police officer for the duration of your event.

You are responsible for stacking and unstacking tables and chairs, and all guests and vendors must vacate by 11pm (or 1am with the additional fee). Anyone remaining in the space past the end time will result in double cleaning charges.

Decorating is welcome with a few restrictions: no confetti, no glitter, no glitter decorations, no confetti-filled balloons. Balloons must be deflated and thrown away. No tape on walls or floors.

There is no full kitchen, but we do have a sink and refrigerator. Crockpots and aluminum trays with sternos work great for DIY food setups. Catering can be hired with no restrictions.

Yes! There is a dedicated bridal room equipped with 4 mirrors, a couch, and several chairs.

Yes — Canton's twinkle lights make a stunning backdrop, and the Courthouse stairs are a popular photo spot. The gazebo can be reserved for pictures through the City of Canton. Several brick walls in the area also make great backgrounds depending on the time of day.

Parking is available on West Main Street — most guests should enter the boardwalk area between Queenie's and Goin' Coastal (no stairs required). Additional parking is available behind Panera Bread, on Archer St, and at the courthouse.
